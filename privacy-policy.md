# Privacy Policy for Household AI

*Last updated: September 2026*

## Overview

Household AI is a personal assistant app developed and operated by an
individual developer for use by the developer's own family, friends, and
invited users. This policy explains what happens to your information when
you use the app.

## What data is collected

Household AI does **not** collect, store, sell, or share your personal data
with any third party for advertising or marketing purposes.

- **Conversations you send** are transmitted directly to a private AI
  server controlled by the developer, solely to generate a response to
  your message. Conversations are not used to train any AI model, sold to
  any third party, or used for advertising.
- **Files you attach** (documents, audio) are processed only to answer
  your question or generate the content you requested (e.g. a summary or
  an audio narration). They are not retained beyond what's needed to
  fulfill your request.
- **Account information**, if you create an account (email, or similar
  identifier), is used solely to identify your session and, where
  applicable, manage your subscription. It is not sold or shared with
  third parties.

## Data storage and retention

Conversation history, where saved, is stored locally on your own device
and/or on the developer's private server infrastructure — not on a public
cloud service accessible to others. You can delete your conversation
history at any time from within the app.

## Third-party services

Household AI relies on the following third-party infrastructure providers
solely to operate the app's core functionality:

- **AI inference providers** (used to generate responses) — your message
  content is sent to these providers only to generate a reply, per their
  own data handling policies. These providers do not use your data to
  train their models on standard API terms.
- **Payment processing** (if you subscribe) is handled by a standard
  third-party payment processor (e.g. Stripe). Payment card details are
  never seen or stored by Household AI directly.

## Children's privacy

Household AI includes optional age-appropriate content settings intended
to help parents/guardians tailor the experience for younger users in their
own household. This app is intended for use within a family or
friend group at the account holder's discretion and is not intended for
independent use by children without a parent or guardian's involvement in
account setup.

## Your choices

- You can delete your conversation history within the app at any time.
- You can stop using the app and request account/data deletion by
  contacting the developer at the email below.
